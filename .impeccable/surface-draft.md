# MediaShelf workspace

Scope: src/App.tsx, src/styles.css, src/components. Visitor mode: operate.
The user explicitly delegated the design and requested a substantial redesign,
animation, bug fixes, and a connected catalog. Existing personal data is authoritative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal editing room for choosing the next story and recording the last one.
The screen leads with a real work of cinema and returns quickly to a usable collection.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-blue workspace, matte charcoal navigation, parchment-white type,
soft copper primary actions, blue secondary states. Manrope for reading, Unbounded
only for short display titles. Real artwork carries visual richness.

STORY: Discover a work, open its details, add it to the shelf, and continue from the
last episode. Local and connected catalogs remain explicit, with real provider states.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 208px navigation rail; an open page heading; a widescreen cinematic
stage with the selected work, poster and direct actions; a filmstrip selector; catalog
controls visible below. Mobile replaces the rail with accessible bottom navigation.

FORM: Grounded direction 3, the editor's screening room. Seed 734bf6e3. The competing
specimen taught scale discipline, the departure board taught stable control positions,
the green field taught full-area color commitment. The book margin, tensegrity graph,
and folded sheet lose both audience identification and task clarity for this catalog.
Their useful disciplines are measured spacing, bounded motion and responsive continuity.
The screening-room direction remains the selected approach under delegated choice.

SIGNATURE: A selected film crossfades into the stage as its poster settles; background
depth follows scroll without hijacking it. The existing real 3D shelf remains an
optional view. Reduced motion uses immediate transitions and a static scene.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
